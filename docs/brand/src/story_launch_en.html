A companion for your Ashtanga practice

Tristhana is a practice log in Telegram. The beta is over: the bot is now open to everyone who practices and teaches.

  • 🧘Practice or rest in one tap. Both go in your log
  • 🌙Moon days for your city, with gentle reminders
  • 📊Your year, your month, your asanas. No competition
  • 👥Teachers: students join by link, a new asana in a few taps
  • 🏛Shalas: schedule, attendance, announcements
  • 🔒Notes and check-ins stay with you
How to start
@tristhana_bot
