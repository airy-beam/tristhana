# ST-07: shareable cards (story 9:16 and square)

- Status: **accepted** (owner: "all as recommended" + Q209–Q211, 2026-11-09, after the ashtanga-expert review) · Date: 2026-11-09 · Size S, Phase 3
- Requirements: **ST-07** (cards: monthly recap, year heatmap, "new asana", "next series begins", "X practices this year"; Telegram `shareToStory` + PNG download). Reuses **ST-01..03** (stats rules), **PR-08** (progression events and celebrations, banned wording), **GM-01** (practice-count ledger), **MD-02** (effective moon location). Owner's idea: "shareable stats, like Garmin".
- Related: ADR-0016, 0020 (cards must project days, never 🌸), 0026, 0027 (bot delivers files to the own chat), 0028, 0032 (moon inheritance), 0036 (capability URL). New: **ADR-0040** (accepted: cards rendered server-side with Pillow, stored 24 h as PNG in Postgres, served at a capability URL on the API; no Cloud Storage).

## 1. Scope
**In:** five card subjects in two formats (story 1080×1920, square 1080×1080), rendered by the backend; a Mini App share sheet (lazy chunk) with preview, **Share to story**, **Save image** and **Send to my chat**; entry points on Stats (month, year), on the 🎉 celebration card, and on given events in My asanas. Asana and series cards for **every series, Advanced A–D included** (Q211).
**Out:** bot entry points (`/stats` later, Q32); a user name, city, teacher name or any free text on cards; photo backgrounds (Garmin-style overlays); `shareMessage` / prepared inline messages to other chats (later, Q207); teacher or shala cards; GM-02 badges; animated cards.

## 2. Cards (what each shows)
Common: one brand palette regardless of Telegram theme (deep indigo background, saffron practice tone, off-white text; `docs/brand/`), brand mark + "Tristhana · @tristhana_bot" footer. Story text stays out of the top/bottom 250 px. **No emoji glyphs** (moon days are drawn rings, the mark is a bundled PNG), so cards look identical everywhere. Language = the account's locale. **Card text never addresses the reader** (no "you/your", «ты/тво…»); it states facts.

| Subject (`kind`) | Shows |
|---|---|
| `month` | "October 2026" (Babel standalone month); headline **"18 practice days in October"** / «18 дней практики в октябре» (Fluent plural; Q210, no "X of Y"); month calendar (Monday first) in **2 tones** + moon rings; "By series": **names only, no counts, in catalog order** (EN "Primary series", RU «Первая серия», as in the catalog), hidden when the only bucket is "short" and never showing "No details"; **"18 h 40 min on the mat"** total when durations exist (never an average). |
| `year` | 12 mini-months heatmap (ST-01 layout) in 2 tones + moon rings. Headline: current year **"2026 so far: 214 practices"** / «С начала 2026 года: 214 практик»; a finished year **"214 practices in 2026"** / «214 практик за 2026 год». Never «…пока». This is also the "X practices this year" card (Q199). |
| `asana` (from a progression event) | **New asana:** "New asana" / «Новая асана» + the asana (EN simple Latin, RU Cyrillic transliteration) + IAST small as metadata + series name + month/year of the event; never "given", "by teacher" or "unlocked". **Series begins:** "Intermediate series begins" / «Начинается вторая серия», subline the traditional name. **Series complete:** "Full Primary series" / «Первая серия целиком», subline "Yoga Chikitsa" / «Йога Чикитса». Series names via the existing `series-name` + `shows_traditional` (as `bots/telegram/progression.py` `series_line`); no "{ $series } серия" template; no traditional name for Advanced A–D. Variant precedence from PR-08's rules: begins > complete > new asana. |
| `count` (GM-01 ledger) | Big **100** + "practices on the mat" (plural); no date, no "year/daily" wording (GM-01 §7 list). |

**Tones (privacy projection, Q209):** practiced → *practice*; every other status (rest, moon, 🌸, sick, travel, auto-rest, pause, "No practice") **and** unmarked → *no practice*. Two tones only, so a 🌸 pattern (a few weekday rest days each month) is unreadable and no "unlogged" signal leaks. **Moon rings** stay, on any cell; a moon day with practice = practice cell + ring, no warning style. Legend: "practice · moon day" / «практика · лунный день». The device's "Show 🌸" switch is ignored. No rest counts, runs, percentages or "in a row" lines (Q201).

## 3. Flows
- **Stats:** a `share-open` "Share" button next to `‹ 2026 ›` (year card) and next to the month title (month card); hidden when that period has 0 practices.
- **Celebration card** (PR-08/GM-01, initial bundle): becomes a `div` with a dismiss tap area and a small "Share" button (no nested buttons). Shown for `new_asana`, `series_begins`, `series_complete`, `practice_count`; not for `item`.
- **My asanas:** "Share" on each given event (newest 20 already listed).
- **Share sheet** (`screens/share/ShareSheet.tsx`, `React.lazy`): segmented **Story | Square** (default Story where `shareStory` is available, else Square) → skeleton → `<img>` preview of the server PNG. Actions: MainButton **Share to story** (story format and `shareStory.isAvailable()`, i.e. mobile clients) with the `text` caption and, only if `initData.user.is_premium`, `widget_link {url: t.me/<bot>, name: "Tristhana"}`; **Save image** (`downloadFile(url, file_name)`, Bot API 8.0+, hidden where unavailable); **Send to my chat** (always; asks `requestWriteAccess` first as ST-09 does; the bot sends the PNG as a photo, so it can be forwarded or saved on any client, desktop included). A one-line privacy note under the preview.
- **App copy** (`app.ftl`, en/ru, «ты»): `share-open` "Share" / «Поделиться», `share-title` "Share a card" / «Поделиться карточкой», `share-format-story` "Story" / «История», `share-format-square` "Square" / «Квадрат», `share-to-story` "Share to story" / «В историю», `share-save` "Save image" / «Сохранить картинку», `share-send-chat` "Send to my chat" / «Прислать в чат», `share-sent` "Done: the card is in your chat with the bot" / «Готово: карточка в чате с ботом», `share-story-caption` "My practice · @tristhana_bot" / «Моя практика · @tristhana_bot», `share-privacy-note` "Cards show only which days had practice: never your name, notes or rest days." / «На карточке только дни практики: без имени, заметок и дней отдыха.», `share-loading`, `share-failed`, `share-rate-limited` "That's a lot of cards today. Try again tomorrow."
- **Card copy** (`backend …/locales/{en,ru}/card.ftl`): `card-month-practices`, `card-month-time`, `card-by-series`, `card-year-so-far`, `card-year-done`, `card-legend-practice`, `card-legend-moon`, `card-new-asana`, `card-series-begins`, `card-series-complete`, `card-count`, `card-footer`; series names reuse `series-name`. Bot caption `share-card-caption` in `bot.ftl`. Fluent plurals on every count. **Grep test over `card.ftl`:** GM-01 §7 list + "unlock", «разблок», "given", "you", "your", «ты», «тво», «пока».

## 4. Domain, use cases, ports
- **`domain/share_cards.py` (pure):** `CardFormat` (STORY 1080×1920, SQUARE 1080×1080), `CardTone` (PRACTICE, NO_PRACTICE), `card_tone(DayStatus | None)`; frozen cards `MonthCard(year, month, tones, moon_days, practiced, series_present, minutes)`, `YearCard(year, finished, tones, moon_days, practiced)`, `AsanaCard(variant, series, key, month)`, `CountCard(count)`; builders take **already projected** tones plus the moon dates passed in: `month_card(...)`, `year_card(...)` (counts from `domain/stats.py`, ADR-0020), `series_present` = buckets in catalog order, empty when only "short"; `asana_card(event, track)` (PR-08 celebration rules; `None` for a correction, start or item event); `count_card(threshold, seen)`; `is_empty(card)`.
- **`application/share_cards.py`:** `CreateShareCard.execute(principal, subject, format) -> CreatedCard(token, expires_at, file_name)`: loads only what the subject needs (stats: `history` + the moon days of the account's **effective** location, FOLLOW_TEACHER resolved by the MD-02 resolver, 2–3 statements; asana: the event, 1; count: the ledger row, 1), **projects every day through `card_tone` before building**, renders via the port, stores via the repo (rate limit in the same insert). No effective location → no rings (never a Mysuru default; nothing is computed). Policy `can_share(principal, subject)` = owner only (`NotFound` otherwise; a teacher's mark on my own track is mine). `GetShareCardImage.execute(token) -> bytes | None` (no principal: the token is the capability). `SendShareCard.execute(principal, token)` → `Notifier.send_card_photo`.
- **Ports:** `ShareCardRepository.insert(account_id, token_hash, kind, format, png, expires_at, daily_limit) -> Inserted | RateLimited` (one statement), `.image(token_hash, now)`, `.claim_send(account_id, token_hash) -> bool`, `.prune(now)` (tick); `CardRenderer.render(card, format, locale) -> bytes`; `Notifier.send_card_photo(recipient, png, caption)`.
- **Adapters:** `infrastructure/cards/pillow_renderer.py` (the **only** Pillow import, lazy, runs in a worker thread; draws only what the card holds, never reads moon data). Fonts, bundled next to it (OFL, subset ~0.5 MB): **Noto Serif Display** for headlines and asana names, **Noto Sans** for body text. The installed Noto Serif Display Regular has glyphs for Cyrillic (U+0410, U+0401, U+044F) and the IAST letters in U+1E00–1EFF (ṇ ṣ ṭ ḍ ṛ ṝ ṃ ḥ ḷ), checked by glyph name on 2026-11-09. The cmap test over all catalog names decides: if any name fails, asana names switch to Noto Sans and the serif stays for headlines only. Fluent `card.ftl` + catalog names resolved there. `bots/telegram/` sends the photo (`sendPhoto`, `BufferedInputFile`). Tick: `prune` after ledger pruning.

## 5. API
| Method, path | Auth / policy | Request → response |
|---|---|---|
| `POST /api/v1/me/share-cards` | initData; owner | `{format: "story"\|"square", card: {kind:"month", year, month} \| {kind:"year", year} \| {kind:"asana", event_id} \| {kind:"count", count}}` (discriminated union) → **201** `{id, image_url, width, height, file_name, expires_at}`; 404 `not_found` (event not mine / not shareable, count not in my ledger); 422 `invalid_period` (future, < 2000), `nothing_to_share` (0 practices); 429 `rate_limited` (> 50 per rolling 24 h) |
| `POST /api/v1/me/share-cards/{id}/send` | initData; the card's owner | → 200 `{sent: true}`; 404; 409 `already_sent`, `cannot_message` (ST-09 codes) |
| `GET /share/v1/{id}.png` | none: capability (outside `/api/v1`, CORS, OpenAPI) | 200 `image/png`, `Cache-Control: public, max-age=86400, immutable`, `nosniff`; unknown/expired/malformed → the same 404 |

`id` = `secrets.token_urlsafe(16)` (128 bits; the content is meant to be public and lives 24 h); stored as SHA-256 only. `image_url` = `PUBLIC_API_URL` + path. Mini App CSP `img-src` adds the API origin (preview). The `MaskFeedTokens` log filter also masks `/share/v1/***`. Regenerate the TS client.

## 7. Cross-cutting checks
- **Time zones:** periods are local dates in the account zone (provisional allowed, like stats); the current month/year is partial up to local today and uses the "so far" headline; the asana card's month comes from `occurred_on` (a local date). Moon rings are the stored calendar's dates of the effective location (MD-03 rule). No time of day on any card, so no zone label. `expires_at` is a UTC instant. Test: Kiritimati vs Honolulu on 31 Dec creating "this year".
- **Privacy:** owner only; the 2-tone projection happens in `application` before any domain builder (a test asserts nothing but practice / no practice reaches a card, and that a 🌸 month and a plain-rest month render identical tones); no unlogged signal; no name, city, teacher, note or check-in; no user-supplied text or pixels (nothing to abuse as hosting); 24 h TTL, hashed token, cascade on delete; the token is masked in logs.
- **i18n:** card copy in backend `card.ftl` (en/ru parity test, reader-free wording grep), Fluent plurals, Babel month names (RU standalone «Октябрь 2026», «в октябре» via the Fluent string), numbers via `NUMBER`; asana names from the catalog (Latin / Cyrillic + IAST); series names via `series-name`; a font-coverage test over every catalog name and every card string. Mini App copy in `app.ftl`.
- **Responsiveness:** opening the sheet is instant (lazy chunk + skeleton); POST = 2–3 statements + render (target < 400 ms p50 warm server time, measure first); the preview GET is one PK read; Pillow is lazy (cold start unchanged), not preloaded. TanStack caches each `[subject, format]` for 10 min, so toggling formats back costs nothing. Bundle: initial +≈ 0.4 KB (buttons, lazy import, copy; SDK share wrappers live in `telegram/share.ts`, imported only by the chunk), share chunk ≈ 3 KB gzip. Image: Pillow + fonts ≈ +15 MB, measured against the 250 MB budget.

## 10. Build notes and deviations (2026-11-09)
Built on branch `st-07/share-cards`. These notes supersede the sections above where they differ.
- **Moon rings (§4, §8):** read through the same MD-02 resolver as the Stats screen (`moon_follow_of`). That includes its 📍 Mysuru default when nothing was chosen (the existing product rule), so a card always matches the person's Stats screen. The renderer never reads or computes moon data (import contract). ashtanga-expert: acceptable on a public card.
- **Send errors (§5):** ST-09's `409 bot_unreachable`, not `cannot_message`. The Mini App asks for write access and retries once, as the export does. `409 already_sent` reads as sent in the sheet. A failed delivery gives the send back.
- **Feature off:** with an empty `PUBLIC_API_URL`, `POST` returns `503 unavailable` and `/share/v1/` returns 404.
- **Asana card:** judged from the event's own before → after (PR-08 precedence: begins > full > new asana), so any older given asana in My asanas can make a card. Backward or stale events make none.
- **Card copy:** `card.ftl` has its own `card-series-name` / `card-series-begins` / `card-series-complete` instead of `series-name`, because the wording differs ("Primary series", «Первая серия», «Начинается вторая серия»). Advanced cards get a subline with the other name: EN "Third series", RU «Advanced A» (expert). In RU, «в» + month is joined by a no-break space; text wraps only at plain spaces, so grouped numbers («1 214») never split.
- **Rate limit:** a cheap `recent` count runs before rendering, then the check inside the insert. It is a **soft** limit: taps racing each other may overshoot by a few (no lock).
- **Image route:** `Cache-Control: public, max-age=3600` (a card lives 24 h at most), HEAD sends the length, and CORS headers are stripped as for `/ical/`.
- **Rendering:** grids size their cells to the space between the headline and the legend (a wrapped headline or a 6-week month never reaches the footer; pixel test). PNG compress level 3. Measured locally: story about 90–110 ms, square about 60–70 ms, first card about 240 ms (Pillow import). Image +23 MB uncompressed (podman: main 256 → 279 MB; CI measures differently).
- **Mini App:** the sheet is portalled to `<body>`, takes focus, closes on Escape, and returns focus to the button. A new card is drawn each time the sheet opens (`refetchOnMount: "always"`); Story ↔ Square inside one sheet stays cached. Stats Share buttons are named "Share: 2026" / "Share: October 2026". Initial JS 145.0 / 150 KB, share chunk 1.1 KB gzip.
- **Open for the owner:** a moon ring on a practised day is shown (factual, same as Stats); the expert recommends keeping it. "New asana" on an older re-given event (after a correction) means "given on that date".
