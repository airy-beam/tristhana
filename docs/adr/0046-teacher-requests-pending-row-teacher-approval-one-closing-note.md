# ADR-0046: Teacher requests: a pending row in its own table, a link only on the teacher's yes, one closing note from the tick
- Status: accepted (owner, 2026-11-17, with `docs/specs/ID-07-one-link.md`: Q317–Q323 as recommended; the product design was approved on 2026-11-16, Q305/Q308)
- Builds on ADR-0030 (the student consents first), ADR-0031 (consent as a column, the teacher read path), ADR-0033 (shala memberships and roles), ADR-0038 (tick delivery from live rows), ADR-0045 (layered consent). On acceptance, "Amended by ADR-0046" goes into ADR-0030, 0031 and 0033.
- Date: 2026-11-16
## Context
A student who joins a shala by its link shares nothing; a teacher there can follow them only after sending a personal link and a second confirm. ID-07 lets the student name their teacher right after joining, and the owner's condition is that the teacher approves explicitly, with nothing from the log visible before that. A refusal must look to the student exactly like a week of silence.

This is the first record in the system that waits for a second person. ADR-0030 avoided one on purpose: the invite code rides in the callback data, so no pending row exists. That cannot work here. The student's consent has to be held somewhere until the teacher answers, and callback data comes from the client, so a "Yes" button that carried the consent could be forged to link any student. The constraints stay the same: account delete is one cascading statement (ADR-0027), the teacher read path joins `teacher_link` and nothing else (ADR-0031), callbacks are stateless (ADR-0008), Cloud Run gives no work after the response (ADR-0018), and the hot account load must not grow.
## Decision
- **A separate table, `teacher_request`,** primary key `(student_id, teacher_id)`, with the shala it came through, `asked_on`, `quiet_note_agreed`, `created_at`, a surrogate `id` for the ledger key, and `state` in `waiting | declined | withdrawn | approved`. **`teacher_link` gains no column and no pending state**, so every existing reader of links stays correct without a filter, and "nothing before the yes" is a fact of the schema.
- **Approval is one statement:** it flips the request to `approved` and inserts the ordinary link, guarded by the 7-day window, both memberships and both link caps. `linked_on` is the request's `asked_on` (the student's local date at the ask, which is what "from today on" in the consent meant); `created_at` is the approval instant, so the default main teacher, list order and announcement audience follow the moment the link came to exist.
- **Consent rides on the row.** The student's tap agrees to a full text with the same items as the invite consent. The flags it grants are stored on the request and copied by the approval, never set by it. A later consent column on `teacher_link` gets a twin here and a new callback letter.
- **Foreign keys go to the two accounts and the shala, all `ON DELETE CASCADE`.** Membership and role are a read-time join: a request counts only while the student's and the teacher's membership rows exist, are older than the request, and the staff row holds the Teacher role. Leaving, removal and role changes therefore need no cleanup write, and a rejoin never revives an old request.
- **The row is also the rate limit.** It stays 30 days whatever its end, so a pair has one request, and a teacher one message, per 30 days. A student may have 3 waiting, a teacher 100; waiting requests count toward the 20-teacher and 500-student caps.
- **Expiry is a read-time rule** on `created_at + 7 days`; nothing is written when a request expires.
- **A decline is stored but projected away.** One domain predicate serves every student-facing surface and treats `declined` as `waiting`. Both silent outcomes become due at the same instant, `created_at + 7 days`, whenever the teacher tapped.
- **Closing notes come from the tick and the ledger** (category `request`, key `req:<id>`): "confirmed" as soon as the link exists, "no answer yet" at day 7. They are computed from the live rows, with no outbox. All messages in this feature are silent, so quiet hours decide nothing. The teacher's note at the student's tap is a direct silent message outside the ledger, as ID-04's join note.
- **Removing a link also closes the pair's request,** as one more CTE in `unlink`, so an old "Yes" button cannot bring back a link someone removed.
- **Only the asked teacher answers.** No owner, manager or other staff member sees a request. Only the Teacher role can be asked.
- **No moon write and no Keep offer.** An approver is always on a shala's staff, and never-chose staff follow their shala with or without students (ADR-0033), so a first student through this path changes nothing for them. The student's city can move only in the two cases the join consent and "Follow my teacher" already promise; as changes made by another person's tap they are silent (ADR-0032).
## Consequences
- The teacher view, marks, announcements, moon inheritance and `teaches` need no change and cannot leak a waiting student. A test pins that a request grants none of them.
- A quietly declined student waits the full week for the note. Any earlier note would mean "declined", so this is the cost of the two cases being the same.
- An approval can make up to 7 days of already-logged days visible at once. The consent says "from today", and the 7-day window bounds it.
- The student's "confirmed" note arrives within a tick (up to 10 minutes), not instantly. In exchange the teacher's tap makes no outbound call, and "Confirm all" for a full room is one statement.
- `declined` sits in plain text in the row for at most 30 days. It is in the teacher's export as their own action and never in the student's. A leak test swaps it for `waiting` and expects equal output.
- Every read of requests carries the validity join. A missed join would show a request that can no longer be approved, not anyone's data.
- Account delete stays one statement; three cascade paths reach the table. In the approve statement a foreign-key failure on the link's student side means the student vanished, which the generic "caller's account is gone" mapping would misreport, so the repository handles it.
- The 30-day rule also blocks an honest retry after a mis-tap or a withdrawal. The teacher's personal link remains the way around it.
- The notification category check widens again (the 0005 / 0024 pattern). One more read and prune per tick.
- A full room sends the teacher one silent message per student, as join notes already do. `MAX_WAITING` bounds what can pile up unanswered.
- The Menu does not show a count of waiting requests, because it renders from the account alone. The note, the students screen and the shala card carry it.
## Alternatives considered
- **A `status` column on `teacher_link` (`pending` / `active`):** one table, but every reader of links (teacher view, main teacher, moon, announcements, caps, export, `teaches`) would need a filter, and one missed filter exposes a log before the teacher's yes.
- **No pending row, the consent in the teacher's callback data (the ADR-0030 pattern):** stateless, but callback data is client-supplied; a forged "Yes" would link a student who never asked.
- **Composite foreign keys to the membership rows:** leaving or removal would delete the request with no join needed. But the row would vanish with the membership, and a declined stranger could leave, rejoin by the wall QR and ask again at once.
- **Delete the request on approval, decline or withdrawal:** the least state, but then nothing limits re-asking, the tick has no source for the "confirmed" note, and a deleted row at day 2 versus day 7 would show the student which case they are in.
- **Send the "no answer" note soon after a decline, or after a random delay:** kinder to the declined student, but any note before day 7 can only mean a decline.
- **Direct silent messages for the closing notes:** instant, but expiry has no tap to ride on, and "Confirm all" would put up to 100 sends inside one webhook request.
- **Materialise expiry with a job that flips the state at day 7:** an explicit `expired` state, but a second writer and a window in which the row and the clock disagree. The read-time rule has neither.
- **Keep an expired request answerable until day 30 (Q318):** fits a slow teacher, but a stale consent could take effect weeks later and reach back a month.
- **Let owners or managers approve for a teacher, or see the queue:** fewer stuck requests, but it reveals who picked whom, and the owner's condition is that the teacher approves.
- **A block list:** stops a persistent stranger for good, but ADR-0030 left it out; the 30-day row, the caps and removal from the shala cover the same ground for now.
