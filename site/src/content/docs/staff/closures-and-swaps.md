A holiday, a moon-day swap, another teacher for one morning: how to change a single date without touching the rest.

## Cancel one class

- **In the app:** tap the class on its date, then :ftl[occurrence-cancel]. :ftl[occurrence-restore] brings it back.
- **In the bot:** open the shala's :ftl[schedule-open] and tap :ftl[class-cancel-pick]. It lists the classes of the next 48 hours.

The class stays in the week, marked as cancelled, so nobody wonders where it went.

## A substitute for one morning

Tap the class on its date in the app and choose someone under :ftl[occurrence-teacher]. The week shows their name with "substitute", and in :cmd[classes] both teachers see who is covering.

## Close the shala for a few days

1. In the app, open the shala and find :ftl[closure-title].
2. Set :ftl[closure-start] and :ftl[closure-end].
3. Tap :ftl[closure-add].

All classes on those days show as cancelled, with the reason "shala closed". You can close up to 92 days in a row, starting from today. :ftl[closure-remove] opens the days again.

## Moon days

What happens on a moon day follows the shala's setting: see [Classes and the week](../classes/).

- To hold one class on a moon day anyway, tap it and then :ftl[occurrence-hold].
- If your shala observes the moon day on a different date from the calculated one, hold the classes on the calculated day and cancel them on yours. Members' own moon days and reminders still show the calculated date, so send an announcement as well.

## Swap a moon day

When a moon day falls on a practice day, some shalas hold that day's classes on their day off that week. If yours does, you can set it up here.

1. Find :ftl[swap-title] on the shala's screen.
2. Choose the :ftl[swap-from] and the day to :ftl[swap-to].
3. Tap :ftl[swap-add].

The new day can be the shala's day off, or a day with no class of the same type, up to 6 days away. It can't be another moon day or a day the shala is closed. Only the classes that were cancelled for the moon day or a closure move. :ftl[swap-remove] puts everything back.

## Good to know

- Nobody gets a message about any of these changes. They show in the schedule and in people's calendar subscriptions. For anything that matters, send an announcement too.
- The owner, managers, teachers and the front desk can make these changes.
