## What it does

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Practice and rest, both count" icon="pencil">
    Mysore, Led, self-practice at home: one tap for the day, more detail if you want it. Rest days
    and moon days are part of the log, not gaps in it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Moon days for your city" icon="moon">
    Full and new moon days are calculated for a real place: your teacher's or shala's city, your
    own, or Mysuru. You get a gentle heads-up before each one.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Your teacher, your shala" icon="group">
    Connect with a link. See the classes of all your shalas in your own time zone, and add them to
    your calendar.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Private by default" icon="approve-check">
    Your diary notes are encrypted and nobody else can read them. A teacher sees only a small, clear
    part of your log, and you can check exactly which.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
