If you are listed in the Sharath Yoga Centre teachers directory, you can ask for a line that says so.

## What it is

One line next to your name. It says the name is in the Sharath Yoga Centre teachers directory, and shows the name as it is listed there and the month it was checked. Your students see it on your invite and next to your name in their list.

## Ask for it

1. In the app, open :ftl[settings-open] → :ftl[links-title] → :ftl[listing-open].
2. Fill in :ftl[listing-field-name] and :ftl[listing-field-region]. The level and the note are optional.
3. Tap :ftl[listing-send].

A person looks your name up in the directory by hand, so it can take a few days. The bot sends you the answer.

## What it says, and what it doesn't

- It is one checked fact with a date: the name was in the public directory when it was looked up.
- It is not an endorsement by Sharath Yoga Centre, and Tristhana has no connection with the centre.
- The level is in your own words. The directory shows no levels.
- Many experienced teachers are not in this directory. The line says nothing about a teacher who doesn't have it, and everything here works the same without it.

## Change or remove it

- :ftl[listing-edit] sends new details. If you change the name or the region, the line hides until it is checked again. Changing only the level doesn't hide it.
- You can remove the line on the same screen. Your students stop seeing it, and you can ask again later.
- The listing is looked at again about twice a year. If the name is no longer found, the line hides and the bot tells you. Your students are told nothing.

## Good to know

- The :ftl[listing-open] row shows only while requests are being taken.
- The note you add is read only by the person who checks, and it is deleted once you have the answer.
- If the answer was "can't confirm", check the spelling against the directory and send the details again. You can send once a day.
