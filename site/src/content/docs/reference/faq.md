Short answers to the things people ask most.

## Do I need the app, or is the bot enough?

Either one is enough. They show the same log, so you can use the bot one day and the app the next. The bot is quickest for logging today. The app has the calendar, the statistics and the longer forms. See [The bot and the app](../../start/bot-and-app/).

## I practice at home, without a teacher. Is this for me?

Yes. Most of Tristhana is your own log: practice, rest, moon days and your asanas. Teachers and shalas are there if you have them, and nothing is missing if you don't.

## I forgot to log a few days. What now?

Nothing is lost. In the bot, open :ftl[menu-open] → :ftl[menu-help] and tap :ftl[help-earlier-day], or tap the day in the calendar on the app's :ftl[tab-practice] tab. You can also leave days empty. An empty day is just an empty day.

## Does a rest day interrupt my practices in a row?

No. Rest days, moon days, days off, sick days, travel and 🌸 days don't interrupt the count. See [Rest days, moon days and 🌸](../../students/rest-and-moon-days/).

## Why is my moon day different from my friend's?

Moon days depend on the place, so two cities can differ by a day. Your dates also follow a city: your own, your teacher's or your shala's. :ftl[menu-moon] in the bot shows which one. See [How moon days are calculated](../moon-calculation/).

## The times look wrong

Tristhana shows every time in your own time zone, and it needs your city for that. Open :ftl[menu-open] → :ftl[menu-settings] in the bot, or the gear in the app, and choose your city in the second row. See [Settings and your city](../../students/settings/).

## My reminders stopped coming

- Check that your city is set: reminders start once it is.
- Practice reminders skip moon days, your days off and days you have already logged.
- After ten practice reminders in a row with nothing logged, the bot sends one note and turns them off, so it doesn't nag. Turn them on again under :ftl[menu-help] → :ftl[help-reminder].

## What does my teacher see?

Your name, practice or rest from the day you added them, your practice length and your last asana. After about three weeks with nothing to show, they see one short line saying so. They can also mark a new asana for you, and you can undo it. They never see your check-ins, diary notes or the reason for a rest. See [My teacher](../../students/my-teacher/).

## How do I pick my teacher at my shala?

In the app, open the :ftl[tab-shala] tab: while you have no teacher, :ftl[request-open]{from=app} is the first row. In the bot, it is on the shala's card. See [My teacher](../../students/my-teacher/).

## I picked my teacher at the shala. What happens now?

Your teacher gets one message with your name and confirms. Nothing from your log is shown before that. If there is no answer after 7 days, the bot tells you: ask them in class, or pick another teacher. See [My teacher](../../students/my-teacher/).

## How do I delete everything?

:ftl[menu-settings] → :ftl[settings-more-button] → :ftl[settings-data-button] in the bot, or the gear → :ftl[settings-more-button] in the app. You can download your data first. See [Your data](../../students/your-data/).

## Where do I find this guide again?

In the bot, open :ftl[menu-open] → :ftl[menu-help] and tap :ftl[help-guide]{from=bot}. In the app, tap the gear :ftl[settings-open] and then :ftl[guide-open]. Both open the guide in your language.

## Something isn't working, or I have an idea

Tristhana is open source. You can open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/airy-beam/tristhana/issues).
