New things in Tristhana, newest first.

## November 2026

- **Ready for launch.** The shala's card lists its classes, and in the bot :ftl[shala-classes-edit] opens them in the app. A different time is another class: save one, then :ftl[class-add-another]. The two invites have clear names, :ftl[shala-invite-students] and :ftl[links-invite]. The bot asks which day a tap on an old Today message is for, and asks which city you live in. Today has :ftl[log-another-day], and the Menu has :ftl[menu-asanas].
- **Clearer words, the same in the bot and the app.** Diary is now :ftl[diary-button], Check-in is :ftl[checkin-edit], Check-ins over time is :ftl[trends-open], Classes is :ftl[menu-classes], Share is :ftl[invite-send], and Length and Duration are :ftl[practice-length]{from=bot} and :ftl[practice-duration]{from=bot}. Times show your city, like 07:00 Almaty, instead of a time zone.
- **The bot points to buttons, not commands.** Its messages say where to tap, for example :ftl[menu-open] → :ftl[menu-moon]. The commands still work as shortcuts: see [All bot commands](../commands/).
- **What there is to do comes first** on the :ftl[tab-shala] tab: :ftl[request-open]{from=app} if you have no teacher yet, or, if you teach, the people waiting for your answer. Your students are one row, :ftl[link-students-open]{from=app}.
- **Pick your teacher in the app too**, in the same words as the bot, on the :ftl[tab-shala] tab or the shala's card. :ftl[request-withdraw-short] takes a request back.
- **:ftl[ann-announce] on a shala's card in the bot** opens that shala in the app, with the form on top.
- **The Practice tab opens on one month.** Four marks: :ftl[stats-legend-practiced], :ftl[stats-legend-rest], :ftl[status-moon] and :ftl[status-skipped]. Tap a day to open it right there. The year is one row down.
- **Settings in the app are the bot's five rows**: what is set shows right on the row. Quiet hours are under :ftl[settings-reminders-button]; your name and your data are under :ftl[settings-more-button].
- **:ftl[settings-open-app]** in the bot now opens the app's settings.
- **Pick your teacher at your shala.** After you join a shala, the bot asks :ftl[request-ask] Tap a name, and your teacher confirms. Nothing from your log is shown before that.
- **For teachers: :ftl[request-yes-mine]** when a student of your shala picks you. Everyone waiting for your answer is in one list at the top of :ftl[menu-students]. Your own link works as before.
- **A shala in two fields**: its name and its city. You start as its teacher, and as the owner you can now change your own roles under :ftl[shala-staff-title]{from=app}.
- **First steps for a new shala**: a short list on the shala's screen. Add a class, invite students and, if you want to, invite staff.
- **A shorter class form**: the type, the start and the days. The rest is under :ftl[class-form-more].
- **Change a class**: tap it in the schedule on the date the change should start from and choose :ftl[class-edit] there. Earlier dates stay as they were. :ftl[class-end] ends a class the same way.
- **:ftl[occurrence-tell]** after you cancel a class on one date: an announcement with the text already written.
- **The app in three tabs.** :ftl[tab-today] asks how the day went, with three choices, and suggests one next step. :ftl[tab-practice] has your calendar, badges and asanas. :ftl[tab-shala] has your teachers, students and shalas. The settings moved behind the gear :ftl[settings-open].
- **A simpler bot.** The first time, it asks :ftl[welcome-ask] After that it opens on today, and everything else is under :ftl[menu-open]. There are no commands to remember: the old ones still work as shortcuts.
- **Three buttons after a practice**: :ftl[practice-same-as-last]{from=bot}, :ftl[practice-add-details] and :ftl[practice-done]. The :ftl[checkin-edit]{from=bot} button now opens the questions.
- **:ftl[menu-help]** is a list of things to do, one tap each. It replaces the quick tour, in the bot and in the app.
- **Settings in five rows**, and shorter screens for invites and joining, with :ftl[consent-details] for the full text.
- **:ftl[practice-same-as-last]{from=bot}** now skips a Led class: after a Led day it offers the practice you did before it.
- **:ftl[trends-open]**: the averages of your answers after practice by month, by weekday and around full and new moon. Yours alone.
- **This guide**, in English and Russian.
- **The quick tour** now covers teachers and shalas.
- **Teachers and students.** A teacher shares a link, a student confirms, and the teacher sees a small card: practice or rest, practice length, the last asana.
- **A teacher can mark a new asana** for a student, and the student can undo it.
- **Shalas**: staff with roles, a weekly schedule, closures, moon-day swaps and substitutes.
- **My schedule**: the classes of all your shalas by your own city's clock, and a calendar subscription for Google, Apple or Outlook.
- **Attendance**: mark the classes you came to, or let the shala's staff mark them.
- **Announcements** from teachers and shalas, which you can mute.
- **Moon days can follow** your main teacher or your shala.
- **Badges, share cards and the asana timeline.**
- **The SYC directory line** for teachers listed in the Sharath Yoga Centre directory.

## October 2026

- **The first version**: log practice or rest in one tap, with the practice length, the kind of class and the duration.
- **Moon days** for your city, with reminders.
- **A daily practice reminder** at the time you choose.
- **My practice**: the year calendar, the month and practices in a row.
- **Rest over several days**: sick, traveling or 🌸, with reminders paused.
- **A weekly day off** that fills in by itself.
- **Check-in and diary note** after practice, private to you.
- **My asanas**: your last asana and small celebrations.
- **My city** in the settings, for times and local moon days.
- **Download your data and delete your account.**
- **The quick tour.**
