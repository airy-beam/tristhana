New things in Tristhana, newest first.

## November 2026

- **Check-ins over time**: the averages of your after-practice check-ins by month, by weekday and around full and new moon. Yours alone.
- **This guide**, in English and Russian.
- **The quick tour** now covers teachers and shalas, in the bot and in the app.
- **Teachers and students.** A teacher shares a link, a student confirms, and the teacher sees a small card: practice or rest, practice length, the last asana.
- **A teacher can mark a new asana** for a student, and the student can undo it.
- **Shalas**: a team with roles, a weekly schedule, closures, moon-day swaps and substitutes.
- **My schedule**: the classes of all your shalas in your own time zone, and a calendar subscription for Google, Apple or Outlook.
- **Attendance**: mark the classes you came to, or let the shala's team mark them.
- **Announcements** from teachers and shalas, which you can mute.
- **Moon days can follow** your main teacher or your shala.
- **Badges, share cards and the asana timeline.**
- **The SYC directory line** for teachers listed in the Sharath Yoga Centre directory.

## October 2026

- **The first version**: log practice or rest in one tap, with the practice length, the kind of class and the duration.
- **Moon days** for your city, with reminders.
- **A daily practice reminder** at the time you choose.
- **My practice**: the year calendar, the month and practices in a row.
- **Rest over several days**: sick, traveling or 🌸, with reminders paused.
- **A weekly day off** that fills in by itself.
- **Check-in and diary note** after practice, private to you.
- **My asanas**: your last asana and small celebrations.
- **My city** in the settings, for times and local moon days.
- **Download your data and delete your account.**
- **The quick tour.**
